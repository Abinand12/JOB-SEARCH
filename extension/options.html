Connect Job Autopilot

In your dashboard, open Bot settings and create an extension token. Paste it here with the dashboard's address.

How it works

  • Click Start auto-apply on LinkedIn in the toolbar popup. The bot searches with the keywords from your dashboard and applies job by job.
  • Jobs that send you to the company website open in a new tab and are filled in automatically.
  • If a site shows a CAPTCHA or asks you to sign in, the bot pauses, shows a notification here and an alert on the dashboard. Complete it in that tab and the bot continues.
  • On any other job site, open the application form and click Autofill this page.
